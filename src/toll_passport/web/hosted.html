How your information stays private

Your information is encrypted in this isolated page before it is saved. The host stores ciphertext, vault scope and storage metadata. Your recovery key and unlocked information stay inside this page. A compromised device, browser extension or malicious software update can read unlocked information. This software has not been independently security-audited. Business team sharing is not included.

Keep your recovery file secure: it contains the key to your book. The host cannot reset it. To restore after storage loss, you need both your encrypted backup and recovery file. Removing a vault cannot recall downloaded copies or erase operator backups.
